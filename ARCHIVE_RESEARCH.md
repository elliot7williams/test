# Recovery notes — NightcoreMaker.com

## What can be verified from surviving external references

- On May 26, 2018 a user comment on AlternativeTo describes `Nightcoremaker.com` as a site where you upload an MP3 and it automatically makes a nightcore version, with minimal options: https://alternativeto.net/software/the-wub-machine/about/
- An August 15, 2018 Urbania article recommends `Nightcoremaker.com`: https://urbania.ca/article/petit-guide-dintroduction-au-nightcore
- A March 27, 2022 comment on r/nightcore's community link roundup says the URL was alive but might soon go down, and refers to a message on the website: https://www.reddit.com/r/nightcore/comments/f4qrfl/nightcore_faq_links/
- Russian-language osu! community discussions linked to other tools hosted at `nightcoremaker.com` in 2016–2017. This does **not** establish the site operator's nationality: https://2ch.life/a/arch/2016-05-12/res/2896689.html

## User's firsthand memories (not independently confirmed from an archive)

- User uploads appeared in a public recent-creations list.
- Posts lasted about 24 hours or dropped off when replaced by new creations.
- A voluntary hosting-donations appeal existed.
- The user recalls an association with Russia.
