03 / THE STORY

A little piece of
old internet magic.

Remember when a simple website could turn your favourite track into nightcore, and everyone could listen to the latest creations? This is an unofficial, independently rebuilt tribute to that experience.

The original NightcoreMaker.com is no longer available. Its complete historic code and design have not been recovered. This recreation uses original code, graphics and sounds, preserving the core idea.
